Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

This application is a personal file-sync setup used only by its owner, William Bostwick. It accesses only the owner's own Google Drive account, with the owner's permission, to copy files between that account and the owner's computer.

No data is collected from anyone else. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Files and account tokens stay on the owner's computer and in the owner's Google Drive.

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account security settings.
